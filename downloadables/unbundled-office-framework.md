# The unbundled office framework

## 1. Remove the daily obligation

Eliminate routine commuting, mandatory attendance, and permanent co-location.

## 2. Retain digital coordination

Keep meetings, email, Slack, and project-management systems.

## 3. Identify what disappeared

Name the valuable functions that used to happen through proximity, including trust, context, informal conversation, and serendipity.

## 4. Rebuild selectively

Use temporary gatherings only where face-to-face interaction adds meaningful value.

## 5. Design intentionally

Define the goal, participants, environment, facilitation, and logistics.

## 6. Return to distributed work

End the gathering and resume remote work rather than turning the retreat into a permanent workplace.
