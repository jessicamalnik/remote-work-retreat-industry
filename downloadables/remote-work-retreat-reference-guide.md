# Remote work retreat industry reference guide

## Central thesis

Remote work did not eliminate the office. It unbundled it.

## What stayed digital

- Meetings.
- Email.
- Slack.
- Project management.

## What companies began buying back

- Trust.
- Relationships.
- Context.
- Psychological safety.
- Alignment.
- Momentum.
- Shared meals.
- Informal conversation.
- Serendipity.
- Time for deeper problem-solving.

## Named retreat companies

- Surf Office.
- BoomPop.
- Offsite.
- TeamOut.
- Moniker Partners.
- Flok.
- WorkTripp.
- NextRetreat.

## Adjacent models

- CEO peer groups.
- Founder masterminds.
- Digital nomad communities.
- Coliving companies.
- Writing retreats.
- Creator retreats.
- Leadership retreats.
- AI retreats.
- Wellness retreats.
- Retreat consulting and education.

## Core distinction

Permanent proximity gives people a place to work repeatedly.

Intentional proximity brings people together for a defined purpose, then ends.
