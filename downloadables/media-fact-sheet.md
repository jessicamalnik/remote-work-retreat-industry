# Media fact sheet

## Project

Remote Work Retreat Industry

## Documentary

*I Investigated Why People Pay Thousands To Recreate The Office*

## Creator

Jessica Malnik

## Core argument

Remote work did not eliminate the office. It unbundled it. Companies kept the digital parts of work and began buying back trust, relationships, context, and informal interaction through temporary gatherings.

## Named companies

Surf Office, BoomPop, Offsite, TeamOut, Moniker Partners, Flok, WorkTripp, NextRetreat, WeWork, Remote Year, Selina, and Hacker Paradise.

## Named interview participants
