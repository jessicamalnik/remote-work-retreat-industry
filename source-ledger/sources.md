## Documentary analysis

These are analytical conclusions made by the documentary:

- Remote work did not eliminate the office; it unbundled it.
- Retreat companies sell intentional proximity rather than permanent proximity.
- The value of a retreat often happens around the scheduled work.
- Identity can connect people after the office stops being the primary connector.
- Retreats and remote work are complements rather than opposites.
- The retreat industry sells back selected office functions that people still value.
